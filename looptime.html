How these numbers work

Polo animations normally play at 24 FPS. totalframe in app.js covers two loops (A + B), so it's always even. A polo that repeats one loop has frames per loop frames in its JSON, and a polo with different A/B loops has totalframe frames.

The result is rounded, so you might need another frame or two, or less. If your FLA project has a different frame count (the number shown below the timeline), use that instead.
